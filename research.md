## Research

- **PhD Candidate** at the University of Milan on the project *Service orchestration in dynamic multi-layer heterogeneous mobile networks*, at CONNETS and OptLab
- **Research fellow** at the University of Milan in the PRIN 2022 project *CAVIA: enabling the Cloud-to-Autonomous-Vehicles continuum for future Industrial Applications*, at CONNETS and OptLab
- **Focus on:**
  - Optimization of computing and network resources in autonomous vehicle systems
  - Microservice placement modeling in cloud-to-edge environments
  - Mathematical programming and heuristic algorithms for combinatorial problems
  - Performance evaluation through simulation environments

## Research Focus

Leading research on **service orchestration in dynamic multi-layer heterogeneous mobile networks** and the **PRIN CAVIA project** (Cloud-to-Autonomous-Vehicles continuum).

- Designed simulation environments for edge-cloud microservice evaluation
- Modeled complex placement problems across cloud-to-edge continuum
- Developed mathematical programming and heuristic solutions for combinatorial optimization

Collaborating with **OptLab** and **CONNETS** laboratories, Dept. of Computer Science, University of Milan.
