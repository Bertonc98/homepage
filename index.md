## About Me

I am Computer Science **PhD Candidate** at University of Milano, in particular I work with the **OptLab** and **Connects Lab**, where I focus on advancing research in network service optimized deployment via Mathematical Optimization and Machine Learning techniques. I worked in the same field as **fellow researcher**.

I also had experience in academic teaching for the the *Informatica e Biostatistica* module (a.y. 2024/2025) in the Veterinary Medicine course, guiding students through information technology and biostatistics concepts, and for the *Computer Networks* cource (a.y. 2025/2026).

From my academic background and industry experience i gained knowledge as **Data Scientist** and **Data Engineer** on different topics, from production field up to theoretical applications.

## Research Interests

- **Microservice placement** and orchestration
- **Combinatorial** and mathematical optimization
- **Cloud-to-edge** systems
- Simulation of distributed computing environments
